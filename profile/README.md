## About Us

RoboteX is a high-tech enterprise committed to a development strategy built on the convergence of artificial intelligence, robotics, and industry. We focus on leveraging large-scale AI model technologies to advance what industrial robots can do — strengthening their multi-dimensional perception, multi-modal cognition, intelligent decision-making, and autonomous execution — while actively exploring the applications of embodied intelligence in industrial inspection.

Our work spans the research, development, manufacturing, sales, and service of intelligent robots, smart monitoring and inspection systems, and control equipment, delivering comprehensive solutions to the industries that keep critical infrastructure running.
